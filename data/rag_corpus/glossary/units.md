# Units of quantity

How farmers' units convert to kilograms on this platform.

## kg

1 kg = 1 kg.

Also written as: kg, kgs, kilo, kilos, kilogram, kilograms, किलो, किग्रा, किलोग्राम, केजी, कि.ग्रा..

## quintal

1 quintal = 100 kg.

Also written as: quintal, quintals, qtl, क्विंटल, क्विटल, क्विं., किंटल, कुंतल.

## ton

1 ton = 1000 kg.

Also written as: ton, tons, tonne, tonnes, टन, टोन.

## bori

1 bori = 50 kg.

Also written as: bori, bories, bora, boras, bag, bags, बोरी, बोऱ्या, बोरे, बोरा, बोरियां, बोरियाँ, बोऱ्यां.

## khandi

The size of one khandi differs from place to place, so it has no fixed value on this platform. Ask the farmer for the quantity in kg, quintals or bags.

Also written as: khandi, khandy, खंडी, खांडी.

## goni

The size of one goni differs from place to place, so it has no fixed value on this platform. Ask the farmer for the quantity in kg, quintals or bags.

Also written as: goni, gony, गोणी, गोनी.

## maund

The size of one maund differs from place to place, so it has no fixed value on this platform. Ask the farmer for the quantity in kg, quintals or bags.

Also written as: maund, mann, मण, मन, मौंड.

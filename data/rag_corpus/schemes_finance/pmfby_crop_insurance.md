# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) crop insurance

## What a farmer pays
The farmer's premium is capped at 2 percent of the sum insured for kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5 percent for rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5 percent for annual commercial and annual horticultural crops. If the actuarial premium is lower than the cap, the farmer pays only the lower amount. The rest of the premium is shared equally by the Centre and the State, except in the North-Eastern states where the split is 90:10.

## Which crops are covered
The scheme covers food crops (cereals, millets and pulses), oilseeds, and annual commercial or annual horticultural crops. Whether a particular crop such as onion, soyabean or tur is covered depends on the state's list of notified crops for your district and season. Ask the bank, a Common Service Centre or the taluka agriculture office.

## Enrolment and cut-off dates
Enrolment is voluntary for all farmers since the kharif 2020 season. A farmer with a crop loan who does not want insurance must give a simple declaration to the bank branch seven days before the cut-off date of enrolment. That rule is from the 2020 government release, so confirm it with your bank. The scheme lists two enrolment cut-off dates for each season: 15 July and 31 July for kharif, and 15 October and 31 December for rabi. Each State or Union Territory can fix the cut-off date for a notified crop according to local weather and crop calendars, so the date for your district and crop is the one that counts.

## How losses are assessed
For widespread yield loss, claims are settled using crop yield data for the notified area, which the State supplies to the insurance company. A farmer who suffers local damage such as hail or flooding should report it to the insurer, bank or agriculture office within the time the scheme allows.

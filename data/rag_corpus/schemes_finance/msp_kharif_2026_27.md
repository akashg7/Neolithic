# Minimum Support Price for kharif 2026-27

## What MSP is
The Minimum Support Price is the price at which the government is willing to buy certain crops, announced before sowing. The Union Cabinet approved MSPs for 14 kharif crops for the 2026-27 marketing season on 13 May 2026. MSP is set at a minimum of 1.5 times the all-India weighted average cost of production.

## Soyabean and tur
Soyabean (yellow) MSP is Rs 5,708 per quintal, an increase of Rs 380. Tur (arhar) MSP is Rs 8,450 per quintal, an increase of Rs 450.

## Onion has no MSP
Onion is not one of the crops with a minimum support price. Onion prices depend on mandi supply and demand, and the government sometimes buys onion through central agencies for price stabilisation at rates it announces separately.

## Using MSP in a sale decision
MSP is a floor that applies only when the government procures the crop. A farmer who can get a mandi price above MSP does not need government procurement. Compare the MSP with the platform price band and the net amount after transport and commission.

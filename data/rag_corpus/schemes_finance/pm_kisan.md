# PM-KISAN income support

## What farmers receive
Eligible landholding farmer families receive Rs 6,000 a year, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 about four months apart. The money goes directly into the family's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer. The scheme started in February 2019 and the central government funds it fully.

## Who is eligible
The scheme covers landholding farmer families. For this scheme a family means husband, wife and minor children. Land ownership is checked against land records. Certain higher-income and institutional categories are excluded under the scheme guidelines, so a farmer who is unsure should check the exclusion list on the official PM-KISAN portal.

## e-KYC is compulsory
A beneficiary must complete e-KYC, otherwise instalments can be delayed or stopped. There are three ways to do it. OTP-based e-KYC works on the PM-KISAN portal and mobile app. Biometric e-KYC is done at a Common Service Centre or State Seva Kendra. Face-authentication e-KYC works in the PM-KISAN mobile app. A Common Service Centre charges a convenience fee of Rs 15 for e-KYC.

## Documents needed
Aadhaar card, bank account details linked to Aadhaar, and land ownership records such as the 7/12 extract or the equivalent record in your state. If the Aadhaar is not linked to the bank account, visit the bank to get it seeded before the next instalment.

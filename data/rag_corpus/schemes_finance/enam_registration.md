# e-NAM registration and selling

## What e-NAM is
e-NAM is the national online market that connects mandis. A farmer brings produce to an e-NAM mandi, the arrival is recorded digitally with a unique lot ID, the quality is assayed, buyers bid online, and payment goes straight to the farmer's bank account. Payment uses RTGS, NEFT or UPI. Farmers can also trade from a warehouse or farmer producer organisation collection centre without bringing produce to the mandi.

## How a farmer registers
Registration is free, there is no fee. There are three ways: on the e-NAM portal, in the e-NAM mobile app, or at the mandi gate during gate entry at the nearest e-NAM mandi. Choose Farmer as the registration type and select your mandi. After the mandi committee approves, you receive a permanent login ID and password. Farmer producer organisations can register in the same ways.

## Documents and details
Basic details such as name, address, date of birth, mobile number and bank details. A bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and a government identity proof.

## Why use it
Transparent online bidding brings more buyers from other markets, quality assaying lets better produce earn a better price, and the sale is recorded with the quantity and price. The e-NAM helpline is 1800 270 0224.

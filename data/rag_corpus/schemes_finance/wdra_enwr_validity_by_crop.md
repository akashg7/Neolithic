# e-NWR shelf life and validity by crop

## Fresh onion
WDRA lists fresh onion as commodity code 23. The declared shelf life is 2 months. An e-NWR for fresh onion is valid for an initial 1 month and can be extended twice, by half a month each time. A pledge loan or warehouse sale on fresh onion therefore has a much shorter window than one on soyabean or tur.

## Soyabean
WDRA lists soyabean as commodity code 13. The declared shelf life is 12 months. The e-NWR is valid for an initial 6 months and can be extended twice, by 3 months each time.

## Tur (arhar, red gram)
WDRA lists tur split (husked) as commodity code 7 and tur whole as code 49. For both, the declared shelf life is 12 months, the initial e-NWR validity is 6 months, and it can be extended twice by 3 months each time.

## Why this matters for the sale decision
If a farmer plans to hold produce and borrow against it, the receipt must still be valid on the day the loan or sale completes. WDRA can revise declared shelf lives, so confirm the current figure on the WDRA commodity page or with the warehouse.

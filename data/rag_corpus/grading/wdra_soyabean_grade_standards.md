# WDRA soyabean grade standards

## Grade-I, Grade-II and Grade-III
Limits are percent by weight unless stated.

| Parameter | Grade-I | Grade-II | Grade-III |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil content, dry basis, minimum | 20 | 18 | 15 |
| Acid value of oil, maximum | 3 | 4 | 6 |
| Moisture, maximum | 10 | 12 | 12 |
| Damaged, discoloured or insect-infested beans, maximum | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| Immature or shrivelled beans, maximum | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| Splits, brokens and cracked beans, maximum | 5 | 10 | 20 |
| Inorganic foreign matter, maximum | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| Organic foreign matter, maximum | 0.5 | 0.5 | 1.5 |

## Range designations
Range-1, Range-2 and Range-3 use essential parameters: oil content minimum 20, 18 and 13 percent; moisture maximum 7, 9 and 12 percent; foreign matter maximum 1.5, 2 and 3 percent; damaged and discoloured seeds maximum 3, 5 and 7 percent; other edible seeds maximum 1, 3 and 6 percent; weevilled seeds maximum 3, 4 and 5 percent by count.

## Other limits
Green seed is not more than 7 percent and protein not less than 34 percent where a processor asks for those optional parameters. Uric acid is not more than 100 mg per kg and aflatoxin not more than 30 micrograms per kg.

## Practical meaning for a farmer
Soyabean moisture at or below 10 percent is needed for the best grade, and 12 percent is the ceiling for Grade-II and Grade-III. Drying the crop before sale or storage is the cheapest way to move up a grade.

To win the Smart India Hackathon with an agritech and price forecasting pitch, you must prove your machine learning accuracy with real data and present a clear logistics plan that replaces the physical role of the middleman.
Analyzing past winning decks reveals specific design patterns, technical proofs, and storytelling structures that separate champions from regular finalists.
## 1. Proving the Price Forecasting Engine

* Show the graph, not just text: Do not write "We use AI to predict prices." Show a sample output graph comparing historical Agmarknet data against your model’s 30-day forecast line.
* Mention exact metrics: Judges look for technical depth. State your model type (e.g., LSTM or XGBoost) and your accuracy score or Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) value.
* Highlight data sources: Explicitly write on the slide that you pull data live from government endpoints like the data.gov.in Open Government Data Platform or AGMARKNET.

## 2. Solving the Middleman Vacuum

* Acknowledge the agent's utility: Middlemen do not just control prices; they provide instant cash and transport. Your slides must explain who replaces this.
* Introduce FPO integration: Show how Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) or local cooperative societies act as your trusted collection hubs instead of chaotic open markets.
* Highlight instant credit/escrow: Show a workflow diagram of instant digital payment split via UPI or escrow so the farmer does not miss the traditional "cash-in-hand" security of a local broker.

## 3. Designing for the Real User (Low Digital Literacy)

* Showcase vernacular/voice UI: Judges reject apps that expect a marginal farmer to navigate a complex English dashboard. Put screenshots of a WhatsApp chatbot, regional voice-based IVR, or a 3-tap regional language screen.
* Keep text minimal on UI slides: Use actual mockups of mobile screens rather than generic vector art.

## 4. SIH Winning Presentation Do's and Don'ts

| Feature | Winning Strategy | Losing Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Slide | Use local data showing exact farmer loss percentages (e.g., 35% margin theft). | Generic statements like "Farmers are sad and poor." |
| Tech Stack | Name specific libraries, frameworks, and APIs. | Vague tech labels like "Advanced AI and Cloud." |
| Business Model | B2B commission, enterprise FPO tier, or logistics fee. | Relying on charging the poor farmer subscription fees. |
| Prototype | Include working prototype video links or live screenshots. | Only theoretical wireframes and text blocks. |

## 5. Pitch Delivery and Q&A Preparation

* Prepare a 2-minute backup slide appendix: Judges will ask about internet connectivity issues in rural areas or transport failures. Have 2 extra slides ready for "Offline USSD fallback" and "Last-mile cold storage failure management."
* Focus on social impact first: Remind judges that a 15% better price realization saves families from debt traps, making the economic argument a moral one.
